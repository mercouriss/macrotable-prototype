# V3.5 audit: product candidate pass

Written 2026-09-29, **before** implementation, against V3.1 (`a0fef35`) and the *V3.5 Product Candidate Master Instructions*. Every proposed change was checked against seven questions: core value, believability/usability, assignment evidence, treatment logic, honest provenance, clutter, and pre-pilot risk.

## Decisions taken with the team (2026-09-29)

1. **Frozen study dataset.** The public product shows every demo brand. Baseline and treatment **trials** use only the three calibrated brands (FitKitchen, Urban Bowl, Local Grill). This keeps the scenarios, the canonical answers and [scenario-difficulty.md](study/scenario-difficulty.md) valid.
2. **Pricing concept:** Free, plus **Premium at €7.99/month (€59.99/year)**, labelled "Prototype pricing / concept — no billing". The team's split:
   - **Free:** core restaurant/menu nutrition browsing and basic tracking.
   - **Premium:** the full AI agent, camera-based recognition, personalised plans, advanced insights.

## Brief audit: KEEP / REFINE / DEFER / REJECT

### KEEP
- **Restaurant density.** Add ~16 verified real restaurants (**19 in total**), identity and location only. Add **3 public demo brands (6 in total)**, spread across integration levels L3/L2/L1 and different cuisines and prices.
- **Frozen study scope.** Trials (and `/baseline`) see only the 3 calibrated brands. The public product sees everything.
- **One recommendation hero.** MealDetail answers WHAT, WHY, CHANGE, CONFIDENCE, PRICE and ACTION at a glance ("Best feasible match", target-vs-meal bars, "MacroTable changed:", provenance, price, *Configure order*).
- **Restrained macro visual** (`MacroFit` bars with a target marker). It is reused on the recommendation, agent cards and review. The existing table stays behind "Details".
- **Home as a product home.** One primary CTA (*Find my next meal*), compact remaining-macro context, "Good options nearby" (best fit per demo store), and a fast route to MacroAgent. Scans become secondary.
- **Explore:** search, and filters the data can support (*Best fit · High protein · Within budget · Vegetarian · MacroTable Demo · Real restaurants*). Pins are dots, with a label only for demo brands and the selected pin. List ↔ map stay in sync.
- **Real restaurants say "Scan the menu to check your fit".** No fit claim is ever made without menu data.
- **Agent recommendation card:** a "Why this fits" line, the fit bars, and one primary action (*Configure order*).
- **Consumer depth, minimal:** **Saved meals** (local, one key, clearable) and **Order again** from order history.
- **Premium concept screen** reachable from Profile, with the Free vs Premium table, price, prototype label and no billing.
- **Hide research/presenter controls** from the normal Profile behind a collapsed "Prototype & research" section. The desktop showcase keeps its presenter controls (brief §26).
- **Copy pass** as the brief describes: one disclosure per screen, details on tap, and no integration jargon in consumer flow ("Full kitchen integration", "Menu & nutrition data", "Menu only").

### REFINE
- **Premium feature split.** The price and positioning are adopted as given. In the prototype, **nothing is actually locked**; Premium is shown as "included in this beta", because:
  1. Gating MacroAgent would remove the treatment from the experiment.
  2. Brief §18 forbids crippling Free to manufacture willingness to pay.
  3. Evaluation needs the full mechanism running.

  Features are labelled honestly:
  - **In the prototype:** MacroAgent, menu-photo reading, saved meals.
  - **Concept (not built):** plate/food photo recognition, personalised meal plans, advanced insights.

  "Basic tracking" in Free is labelled as sample data, because the prototype does no real food logging.
- **Filters** apply only where data exists. *High protein*, *Best fit* and *Within budget* evaluate menu-carrying (demo) restaurants only; real restaurants are excluded and the reason is stated.
- **OFFICIAL.** The brief lists plain `OFFICIAL`. The V3.1 rule stands: all simulated data is labelled `DEMO OFFICIAL`, and plain `OFFICIAL` is reserved for genuinely published data (none exists yet).
- **Agent store list** with 19 real places: the tool returns demo stores plus the **3 nearest** real ones and a count. This keeps the model payload and the card small.
- **Map framing** fits the demo brands and "you". Real restaurants further away are reached by panning or from the list.

### DEFER
Everything in brief §31, plus:
- plate/food recognition;
- personalised meal plans;
- advanced insights;
- real geolocation;
- accounts and cloud sync of saved meals;
- dark mode;
- full-text dish search across real restaurants (there is no data to search);
- a separate comparison screen (the stores card and ranked list already compare).

### REJECT
- **Paywalling MacroAgent or scanning** inside the prototype. It breaks the experiment and the Free value demonstration; shown as a concept only.
- **New demo brands in study arms.** Team decision; it would invalidate scenario calibration.
- **Any fit or nutrition claim for real restaurants** before a scan, including in filters and sort.
- **Scraped restaurant photos or logos.** Demo brands get original monograms; real ones get a neutral initial.
- **Sponsored/commission-weighted ranking.** Ranking stays fit-first and independent of integration level (unit-tested).

## Research-validity guardrails for this pass
- The baseline UI is not changed, apart from reading the explicit study dataset.
- Scenario targets, the optimizer, feasibility rules and outcome definitions are unchanged.
- The freeze fingerprint changes, because the prompt and tool descriptions changed. **V3.5 is a new treatment:** do not pool it with V3/V3.1 observations.

## Implementation record

### Restaurant verification (2026-09-29)
Method:
1. Query OpenStreetMap (Overpass) for `amenity=restaurant|fast_food|cafe` within 1.6 km of the demo location.
2. For each candidate with a website: fetch the site, confirm it is live, and confirm it states the same address as the OSM object.
3. Keep only candidates that pass both checks.

Candidates were dropped when the site timed out (In Den Rustwat, Eetcafe Concordia, Osteria Vicini), returned 403 or showed no address (The Yan's, STACH, Japita, Light of India, Hua Hin), or showed no street name (Maaskantine).
