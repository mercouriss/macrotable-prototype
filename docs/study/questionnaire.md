# Post-task questionnaire

Ask verbally or on paper/tablet **after each task**, using the identical wording each time. Record answers in [`questionnaire-responses-template.csv`](questionnaire-responses-template.csv): one row per participant × condition, integers 1–7, blank if not asked. Never record names or contact details.

Participant: `____`   Task: 1 / 2   Condition: Baseline / MacroTable

## After every task (both conditions)

| # | Question | 1 | 7 | CSV column |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 | How mentally effortful was it to choose your order? | Not effortful at all | Extremely effortful | `effort` |
| Q2 | How confident are you that this order fits the nutritional goal? | Not confident at all | Extremely confident | `confidence` |
| Q3 | How satisfied are you with the order you selected? | Very dissatisfied | Very satisfied | `satisfaction` |

## Additionally after the MacroTable task only

| # | Question | 1 | 7 | CSV column |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q4 | How useful was MacroTable for choosing your order? | Not useful at all | Extremely useful | `usefulness` |
| Q5 | How much did you trust MacroTable's recommendation and nutrition information? | Did not trust at all | Trusted completely | `trust` |
| Q6 | How comfortable would you be letting MacroTable configure your meal, as long as you give final approval? | Not comfortable at all | Completely comfortable | `delegation_comfort` |
| Q7 | How likely would you be to use a tool like this when ordering from a restaurant? | Not likely at all | Extremely likely | `likelihood_use` |

Show the 1–7 scale with only the two ends labelled. Don't explain the questions beyond repeating them.

## Final open questions (once, after both tasks)

Write down answers as close to verbatim as you can. Don't prompt or suggest answers.

1. What was the hardest part of choosing the meal?
2. What, if anything, made MacroTable easier or harder than normal ordering?
3. Was there anything about the recommendation or nutrition information you did not trust?
4. What would have to be true for you to use this with a real restaurant?

In the final evidence register, label open-question findings **INTERVIEWED**, not TESTED.
